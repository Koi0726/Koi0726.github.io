## Research Interests

- Vehicle and Tire Noise and Vibration Method and Practice 
- Topology Optimization of Dynamic Problems with Material Nonlinearity
- High-performance Computing and Optimization Acceleration (ML/Quantum)
- Mechanics and Design of Soft Material Systems

I focus on vibration, wave propagation, and energy dissipation in soft and viscoelastic systems, combining topology optimization with advanced computational methods.

Currently, my work in the tire industry centers on NVH analysis and design, where I investigate how material behavior and structural dynamics influence noise and vibration performance.

My PhD research focused on load control and energy manipulation in viscoelastic structures through dynamic topology optimization, with particular emphasis on material nonlinearity and frequency-dependent behavior.

I am also interested in accelerating large-scale simulations and optimization using high-performance computing and emerging techniques such as machine learning.

Beyond tire NVH and topology optimization, I maintain a broader interest in soft material systems, including areas such as compliant mechanisms and soft robotic concepts, as potential extensions of energy and deformation control in engineering design.
